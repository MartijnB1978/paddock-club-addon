Good to know

  • No prediction, no points.
  • Results come from Formula 1's official timing. Driver of the Day is entered by the admin after F1 announces it.
  • Results can still change for 48 hours after the race (stewards' decisions). After that the weekend is frozen.
  • Equal points at the end: the player who had the best weekend score most often wins.
  • Everyone's picks become visible once the deadline of that part has passed.
